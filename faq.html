About Redevotion

What is Redevotion?

A Scripture-centered companion for personal devotion and family worship. It brings together Bible reading, prayer, catechism, Scripture memory and the church’s historic writings, in an order you set. How it works

Who is it for?

Anyone who means to keep a daily devotion, alone or with a household, with Scripture and the church’s historic words at its center. It suits a first attempt at a daily practice as well as a routine kept for years. Ways to use Redevotion

Does it keep score?

No. It records only what you mark complete, so that it can keep your place. Nothing is counted as missed.

What you can do now, and what is not yet here

Can I download it yet?

Redevotion is in development for iPhone and Android. It is not available yet. When it is released, the official store links will appear on this site.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Try Redevotion, on this site, recreates a short devotion from the app’s own screens in your browser. It collects nothing.

Can a household share Redevotion between phones?

Not in the current version. Family Worship is led from one phone, and each phone keeps its own data. Anything that changes this will be described here when it is available.

Scripture

Which Bible translations are included?

Seven: ESV, KJV, CSB, NASB 1995, NASB 2020, LSB and NKJV. The LSB is the default; you can choose another in Settings.

Is the whole Bible in the app?

The Scripture text of the passages used in devotion parts is built in, within each publisher’s permissions. Bible Reading plans give references, and any passage can be opened in the Bible app or website you prefer. More about Scripture

Where are the translations’ copyright notices?

In the app, in Settings, under About & Legal, Scripture Copyrights.

Devotions and practices

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost, and nothing is counted as missed. The parts you didn’t finish are there the next time you begin.

Why didn’t a part move on?

A part moves on only when you complete it. Opening it, scrolling through it or choosing Continue never counts as completing it. How a devotion moves

What does Finish for Now do?

It closes the devotion. What you completed stays completed, and the rest waits; Resume picks up where you left off.

How does a family use it together?

Family Worship is led by one person from one phone, in an order kept for the household. Each person’s own devotion, prayer list and memory work stay on their own phone. Leading family worship

Making it yours

Can I change the order of a devotion?

Yes. Add, remove and reorder its parts; none is required. A part drawn from the Library can move through its texts in order, through only the ones you choose, or keep the same one. Making it yours

Can I see catechism answers straight away?

Yes. Answers are hidden until you tap, to begin with; the setting can be turned off. How Catechism works

What are Modern and Traditional readings?

The historic prayers, the confessions and three of the catechisms (the Westminster Shorter, the Baptist and the Catechism for Young Children) can be read in plain modern wording or in their traditional wording. A Catechism for Boys and Girls is given as its publisher prints it.

Can passages open in my own Bible app?

Yes. Choose the Bible app or website you already use, and each reference opens there.

Your data and privacy

Where is my data kept?

On your device. Redevotion has no account and no ads, and does not track you. Privacy policy

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account to create, and what you add stays on your device.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup file on the old phone and save it somewhere you can reach, then restore it on the new one. Support has the steps.

Can I move between an iPhone and an Android phone?

That has not been confirmed. Restoring a backup made on an iPhone onto an Android phone, or the other way round, is not yet verified. Back up and restore between phones of the same kind.

Help

Where do I start?

Your first week sets out a simple way to begin, a little at a time. It is optional.

Where can I get help?

Support answers common questions. You can also write to Redevotion@outlook.com.
